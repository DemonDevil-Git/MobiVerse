Privacy · Local-first

Convert ebooks without uploading them

MobiVerse processes books, covers, metadata, and previews on your computer. It is designed for users who do not want to send personal reading files to an online conversion service.

Download MobiVerseInspect the source
Short answerMobiVerse is a desktop converter. Your source books are not uploaded to a MobiVerse server, and converted EPUBs remain in your local filesystem.

What stays local

  • Book classification and format analysis.
  • Conversion and comic page rebuilding.
  • Cover extraction and caching.
  • EPUB preview and reading position.
  • Conversion history and validation reports.

Why this matters

Ebooks can contain private annotations, personal documents, licensed material, or filenames that reveal reading habits. A local workflow avoids adding a web upload step to the conversion process.

Supported local workflows

MobiVerse opens EPUB directly and converts readable MOBI, AZW, AZW3, CBZ, CBR, ZIP, and PDF sources to EPUB 3. The macOS release includes the required conversion components.

Local processing does not grant permission to convert protected or copyrighted material. MobiVerse does not remove DRM.
