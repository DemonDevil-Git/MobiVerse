How to convert MOBI to EPUB without uploading your book

MobiVerse converts readable, DRM-free MOBI files locally and creates an EPUB beside the source file for Apple Books and modern EPUB readers.

Download MobiVerseView source
Short answerInstall MobiVerse, drag in the MOBI file, confirm the detected type, and convert. The source book stays on your computer and the EPUB is saved beside it.

Steps

  1. Download the latest official MobiVerse release.
  2. Open MobiVerse and add the `.mobi` file.
  3. Review the analysis and start conversion.
  4. Open the resulting EPUB in MobiVerse or Apple Books.

When this works

This workflow is intended for DRM-free MOBI files and personal documents that your system can already read. It is useful for old Kindle libraries, public-domain books, and personal archives.

DRM limitation: MobiVerse does not remove or bypass DRM. Protected Kindle purchases may fail to convert.

Why choose MobiVerse

  • No web upload or account.
  • No separate Calibre installation for the macOS release.
  • Built-in EPUB preview and conversion history.
  • Special handling for illustrated books and comics.
