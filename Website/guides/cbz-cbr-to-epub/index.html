Convert comic archives to fixed-layout EPUB

MobiVerse turns ordered comic images into self-contained fixed-layout EPUB pages designed for stable sizing in Apple Books and modern readers.

Download MobiVerse
Short answerAdd a CBZ, CBR, or ZIP archive, confirm that it is a comic and choose the reading direction. MobiVerse rebuilds the images as fixed-layout EPUB 3 pages.

Prepare the archive

  • Use sequential filenames such as `001.jpg`, `002.jpg`, and `003.jpg`.
  • Remove unrelated thumbnails or hidden files when possible.
  • Confirm whether the comic reads left-to-right or right-to-left.

Conversion steps

  1. Add the archive to MobiVerse.
  2. Review the comic classification and reading direction.
  3. Start conversion and wait for validation.
  4. Inspect the EPUB in the built-in paginated preview.

Why fixed layout matters

Comic pages are complete compositions. Fixed-layout EPUB preserves each page as a stable viewport instead of letting a reader reflow or stretch the artwork.
