Fix stretched or black comic pages in Apple Books

MobiVerse rebuilds image-heavy books with fixed-layout metadata, self-contained page markup, cover metadata, and stable image sizing.

Download MobiVerse
Short answerConvert the original comic archive, image-heavy Kindle file, or PDF with MobiVerse. It rebuilds comic pages instead of relying on a generic reflowable EPUB layout.

Problems this workflow targets

  • Pages stretched beyond their original aspect ratio.
  • Black or blank pages caused by fragile markup.
  • Unexpected margins around full-page artwork.
  • Page dimensions changing during horizontal turns.
  • Missing cover and reading-direction metadata.

What MobiVerse changes

For image-page books, MobiVerse writes fixed-layout EPUB metadata and self-contained SVG-backed pages. Images are centered and fitted without changing their aspect ratio.

Recommended workflow

  1. Start from the original CBZ, CBR, ZIP, PDF, MOBI, or AZW3 source when available.
  2. Confirm that MobiVerse identifies the source as a comic or illustrated book.
  3. Choose the appropriate reading direction.
  4. Convert, preview several pages, and then open the EPUB in Apple Books.

Reader behavior can vary by OS version. Keep the original source and test representative pages before deleting an older EPUB.
